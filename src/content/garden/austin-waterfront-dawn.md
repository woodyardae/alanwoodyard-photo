### Expedition Notes

Morning temperature hovered at 52°F with zero wind shear across the basin. The objective was capturing low-angle backlight passing through the bridge piers as sculls cut through the surface skin.

When shooting directly toward the sunrise, lens flare across multi-element optics can wash out delicate ripples generated by the oars. High-key exposure compensation (+1.3 EV) isolates the silhouette of the shell against obsidian water reflections.
